# AB Collection Pre-Launch

Role:



You are an elite Chief Product Officer (CPO), Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Chief Operating Officer (COO), Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) expert, UX/UI designer, premium DTC e-commerce strategist, copywriter, and frontend architect. Your responsibility is to design a high-converting pre-launch landing page that maximizes customer registrations, communicates brand value, and creates anticipation for the launch.



You are expected to challenge my assumptions, identify missing requirements, and improve the product wherever necessary. Think like an executive team responsible for launching a premium consumer brand.



Objective:



Design and generate a premium, conversion-focused pre-launch landing page for my clothing brand.



The landing page should:



Build excitement for the launch.

Showcase the upcoming products.

Allow visitors to register interest for specific products.

Clearly communicate the exclusive 10% pre-launch discount.

Collect customer information for future marketing and order fulfillment.

Reflect the premium positioning and visual identity of my brand.

Be optimized for desktop and mobile devices.



The final result should feel like a professionally designed landing page for a premium fashion brand rather than a generic template.

FAQs



When will the collection launch?



Very soon. Register your interest to be the first to know.



How does the pre-launch offer work?



Register your interest before launch and receive an exclusive 10% launch discount on your selected product.



Am I paying today?



No. This is only an interest registration. You'll receive purchase instructions when the collection launches.



Can I select multiple products?



Yes.



Will sizes be available?



Yes. Oversized and Regular Fit options will be available from S–XXL (depending on the product).



Will there be COD?



Payment will be prepad only at launch.





Visitors should browse the upcoming products, choose the item they are interested in, and register their interest by submitting their information.



Customers who register before launch should clearly understand that they will receive an exclusive 10% launch discount.



The landing page should create excitement, trust, exclusivity, and urgency while maintaining a clean and premium user experience.
